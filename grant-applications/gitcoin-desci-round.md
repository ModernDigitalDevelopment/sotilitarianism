## What We're Building

Sotilitarianism is a new economic philosophy and protocol that proposes a fundamental shift in how we measure and reward value. The core innovation is the **efficiency-backed stablecoin** — a monetary token whose supply is governed by verified organizational efficiency rather than dollar reserves or crypto collateral.

This is not just a protocol — it is a complete economic framework backed by a 117,000-word academic treatise, 20 smart contracts, and a three-token economy designed to align individual incentives with collective outcomes.

---

## The Academic Contribution

The Sotilitarianism framework makes three novel contributions to economic science:

**1. Efficiency-Backed Monetary Theory**  
Traditional monetary theory ties money supply to gold, fiat reserves, or crypto collateral. Sotilitarianism proposes tying it to *verified productive efficiency* — a concept with no direct precedent in academic literature.

**2. The Dual-Lever Economic Model**  
Most economic interventions address either supply-side or demand-side factors. The Dual-Lever Model addresses both simultaneously through tokenized incentive structures, creating a self-reinforcing feedback loop between contribution and reward.

**3. Continuous Consent Governance**  
Traditional democratic theory relies on periodic elections. Sotilitarianism proposes Continuous Consent — an ongoing, on-chain governance mechanism where delegation can be updated at any time, creating a more responsive and accountable form of collective decision-making.

All three contributions are developed in full in the [Sotilitarianism book](../book/README.md) and are being prepared for academic publication.

## Why This Belongs in DeSci

DeSci (Decentralized Science) is about making research open, reproducible, and community-funded. Sotilitarianism embodies these principles:

- **Open source:** All 20 smart contracts are MIT-licensed and publicly available
- **Reproducible:** The economic models are implemented in Solidity — anyone can fork and test them
- **Community-funded:** The Elevation Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit — no private profit extraction
- **Peer-reviewed:** Academic publication of the efficiency-backed stablecoin paper is in progress
