# About the Author

## Cornelius Lawrence

**Cornelius Lawrence** is the creator of Sotilitarianism and founder of The Elevation Foundation, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to building transparent, community-governed financial systems using blockchain technology.

His work sits at the intersection of economic philosophy, blockchain governance, decentralized finance, and community-driven technology. Through Sotilitarianism, he proposes a fundamental restructuring of how economic value is created, measured, and distributed — replacing extractive capitalism with a system governed by verified social utility.

Lawrence's intellectual contributions span multiple domains:

- **Economic Philosophy** — The Dual-Lever Economic Model, the Utility Maximization Feedback Loop, and the concept of Verified Utility as a replacement for speculative value
- **Political Theory** — Continuous Consent governance, Liquid Democracy with real-time recall, and multi-level governance architecture
- **Technical Architecture** — The Five-Layer Sotilitarian System, the Three-Token Economy (SOT/SUG/SST), and a comprehensive smart contract suite
- **Applied Technology** — The Transparently DApp, WeSolar community solar platform, and the Elevation Engine autonomous yield protocol

His writing includes the four-part treatise *Sotilitarianism: A New Economic Order*, the five-part *Sotilitarian Capitalism* manifesto series, *The Sotilitarian Revolt: Rewriting Value in the Age of Trust*, and numerous whitepapers and technical documents.
