## Key Concepts

**The Dual-Lever Synthesis** transcends the traditional Keynesian vs. supply-side dichotomy:

- **Lever 1 (Demand)**: Tokenized rebates (SST), contribution rewards (SUG), ownership distributions (SOT) create broad-based participatory income
- **Lever 2 (Supply)**: On-chain accountability, smart contract automation, and AI verification drive systemic efficiency

The two levers create a **self-reinforcing feedback loop** where participation drives efficiency, efficiency drives value, and value drives further participation.
